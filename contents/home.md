Yin Ye is a specially appointed associate professor at the School of Information Engineering, Jiangxi University of Water Resources and Electric Power. His research interests include Machine Learning, Image Processing, Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Remote Sensing, Evolutionary Algorithm, Engineering Application. Currently, he is served as a peer reviewer over 80 times for 20 conferences and journals, including EI Conferences (ICCVIT 2026, ICECET 2026, ACDSA 2027, etc) and the SCIE journals (remote sensing, IEEE internet of things journal,Computers and Electronics in Agriculture, Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence, Pattern Recognition, etc). And He has served as a Young judge for the CSCIED Technology Core Evaluation Database, PC Member of ICHEC 2026, Editorial Board Member of Scientific Reviews, and Editorial Board Member of the Q1 multidisciplinary SCI journal Scientific Reports. At present, he has authorized 6 Chinese patents, presided over the Early Career Youth Scientific and Technological Talent Cultivation Project of the Jiangxi Province and published a total of 20 academic papers. Among them, published 2 papers as the first author in Q1 journals with an impact factor above 10.
